# EXTREME RACING 3D (Phase 1 - 13)

Godot 4.3 (Mobile renderer), GDScript, landscape Android.

## Phase 1 contains
- Procedural closed-loop track (road with markings, barrier walls with collision, grass, 450 trees, mountains, start arch)
- 3 drivable cars (Sports Coupe, Supercar, Muscle Car): 4 wheels that spin, suspension, steering, brake lights
- Arcade physics (acceleration, brake, reverse, drag, downforce, speed-sensitive steering)
- Chase camera (speed-based distance and FOV)
- Touch controls: left, right, GAS, BRAKE, RESET (back on track), CAR (switch car)
- HUD: speed, lap, lap time, best lap

## Phase 2 adds
- Nitro: meter, extra acceleration + top speed, exhaust flames, FOV/rumble/speed lines; recharges slowly and while drifting
- Drift: handbrake (DRIFT button) lowers rear grip, slip-angle detection, drift score with x1-x5 multiplier, tire smoke, grip recovery, banked score shown on HUD
- Camera: 3 views (VIEW button: near chase, far chase, bumper), crash shake, drift look-ahead
- Crash detection (sudden speed loss) cancels the current drift

## Phase 3 adds
- Main menu: mode, track, car, AI level, laps
- Modes: Quick Race (you + 5 AI, 3-2-1-GO countdown, laps, positions), Time Trial (best lap saved), Free Drive
- AI opponents: follow the track, plan speed for corners, overtake, use nitro on Hard, recover when stuck, light rubber-banding
- 2 tracks (Grand Loop, Technical Circuit), results screen with coins, local save file (coins, best laps)

## Phase 4 adds
- Garage (menu -> GARAGE): 3D showroom with a turntable car. Drag to rotate, pinch / +/- to zoom, < > to browse.
- 8 cars (tiers, cheapest first): Sports Coupe (free), Muscle Car, Luxury Sports Sedan, Supercar, Premium Grand Tourer, Electric Performance, Racing Supercar, Hypercar. Prices are in `car_data.gd`.
- Buy / select cars with coins. The menu CAR button and Free Drive car switch only cycle through cars you own.
- 4 upgrades per car, 5 levels each: Engine (accel + top speed), Tires (grip + braking), Nitro (longer burn, faster recharge), Handling (steering response + grip). Costs 300/600/1000/1500/2200.
- Paint (10 colours) and wheel colour (6) per car. Everything is saved locally per car.
- AI opponents now drive cars from the same tier as yours (+/- 1), so racing a Hypercar is not against Sports Coupes only.
- Note: the old Supercar and Muscle Car are now locked until bought (existing saves keep their coins).
- All 8 cars share the same procedural body shape (different size, colour, spoiler and stats). They look distinct only by proportions until you drop real GLB models in `assets/cars/`.

## Phase 5A adds (audio)
- All sounds are generated by code at start-up in a worker thread (original, no files, no licences). Sounds not ready yet are skipped, so the menu opens instantly.
- Engine: one looping sample, pitch follows a 5-gear rpm model (revs at the start line, drops after each shift + shift pop). AI cars are positional 3D sounds.
- Tyre screech while drifting / handbraking, nitro whoosh, crash hit (stronger = louder), countdown beeps, GO, win / lose jingle.
- Menu + garage music and a faster race track; SOUND and MUSIC buttons in the menu (0/25/50/75/100%), saved in user://audio.cfg.
- Sound is muted while the app is in the background.
- Audio code: `scripts/audio_manager.gd` (autoload), `scripts/car_audio.gd`.

## Phase 5B adds (lighting, environment, optimization)
- `scripts/race_environment.gd`: two time-of-day presets, picked by track. Grand Loop = DAY, Technical Circuit = SUNSET (low orange sun, warm fog, orange sky). Drifting clouds. Glow + colour adjustment on Medium/High (SSAO is not available in the Mobile renderer, so it is not used).
- Sunset: player car gets two real headlight spot lights (Medium/High). Street lamps glow brighter.
- New scenery in `track_builder.gd`: street lamps on both sides, rocks, bushes (plus the trees). All are MultiMesh, split into 12 chunks along the track with a visibility range, so far chunks are not drawn.
- Graphics LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH (menu button, saved in user://graphics.cfg): changes render scale, MSAA, shadows, post effects, clouds, number of trees/rocks/bushes/lamps and particle counts. FPS 30 / 60 button (saved).
- Not done: rain, motion blur, LOD meshes, pause menu, tilt steering, Career mode, maps 3-5.

Not yet built (later phases): Career mode, more maps (city/coast/desert/night), pause menu, tilt steering, full settings screen.

See tests/MANUAL_TEST_CHECKLIST.md.

## Phase 6 adds (build + QA)
- Workflow: debug APK on every push, build logs always uploaded (`build-log` artifact), GDScript errors flagged during import.
- Manual "Run workflow" with `build_aab=true` builds a signed release AAB. Needs secrets `ANDROID_KEYSTORE_BASE64`, `ANDROID_KEYSTORE_USER`, `ANDROID_KEYSTORE_PASSWORD`. Preset "Android AAB" uses Gradle. This path is unverified.
- `tests/TESTING_REPORT.md`: what was checked statically and what is still untested.

## Phase 7 adds (maps, career, pause, tilt)
- 3 new procedural maps (5 total, `scripts/track_data.gd`): COASTAL HIGHWAY (sand island, ocean, palms, bright noon), DESERT RUN (cacti, dunes, haze), NIGHT CITY (skyscrapers with lit windows, neon signs, dark sky, player headlights). Each has its own road shape, collision walls, lamps, sky preset and AI racing line.
- CAREER (main menu): 10 events (`scripts/career_data.gd`). Finish in the required position to pass; passing pays coins, opens the next event and unlocks tracks 3-5 and cars (Muscle Car, Luxury Sedan, Supercar, Hypercar). Progress is saved. Quick Race / Time Trial only offer unlocked tracks.
- PAUSE: II button (top right), Esc on PC, Android back button, and automatic when the app goes to the background. Resume / Restart / Quit, plus tilt settings.
- TILT STEERING (menu or pause): uses the accelerometer, calibrated when the race starts / resumes. Sensitivity 50-200%. If it steers the wrong way use TILT DIRECTION: INVERTED in the pause menu. Touch buttons still work together with tilt.
- Main menu is now two columns so it fits 720p.
- Not done: tunnels, bridges, rain, motion blur, LOD meshes, real GLB cars, customizable control layout, cockpit view.

## Phase 8 adds (rain, tunnels, cockpit)
- WEATHER button in the menu (CLEAR / RAIN, saved). Rain: streaks that follow the camera, greyer sky, thicker fog, weaker sun, and a wet road (cars and AI get about 12% less grip and braking). Particle count follows the graphics quality.
- Tunnels on every track (`tunnels` in `scripts/track_data.gd`): concrete walls and ceiling with collision, ceiling lights, no lamp posts inside.
- 4th camera view: COCKPIT (driver's eye inside the cabin). VIEW button cycles near chase, far chase, bumper, cockpit.
- Not done: rain sound, bridges, motion blur, LOD meshes, real GLB cars. Sun still lights the road inside tunnels on LOW (shadows are off there).

## Phase 9 adds (car shapes, rain sound, fixes)
- Each of the 8 cars now has its own procedural body (`"shape"` in `scripts/car_data.gd`): cabin length / height / position, front and rear fender height, hood scoop (Muscle Car), side intakes, low lip wing (Supercar), high wing (Racing Supercar, Hypercar), rear fins (Hypercar), full-width light bars and no exhaust (Electric). The cockpit camera follows each car's cabin height.
- Rain now has a sound (generated by code like the others, plays only when WEATHER is RAIN).
- Fixed: GDScript type errors that stopped the game from starting (typed loop variables). The build fails on script errors and lists them on the run's Summary page. Fixed signing key so new APKs install as an update.
- Start screen checks all scripts and shows any error on screen; LOG button in the menu shows the engine's error lines.

## Phase 10 adds (menus, rewards, touch layout, perfect start)
- Main menu: CAREER, quick-race setup and START on the left; GARAGE, SETTINGS, PROFILE, REWARDS on the right.
- SETTINGS screen: sound, music, graphics, FPS, tilt steering (on/off, sensitivity, direction), TOUCH BUTTON SIZE (80/100/125%) and CONTROL LAYOUT (standard / left-handed: driving buttons mirrored).
- PROFILE: coins, wins, cars owned, career progress, tracks unlocked, best lap per track.
- REWARDS: daily reward (200 + 100 per streak day, up to day 7; uses the phone's clock, so it can be cheated by changing the date) and win milestones (5 / 15 / 40 wins = 1000 / 3000 / 8000 coins).
- PERFECT START: press and hold GAS in the last 0.6 s of the countdown (not earlier) for +35 nitro and a "PERFECT START!" message.
- Not done: bridges, motion blur, LOD meshes, real GLB cars.

## Phase 11 adds (cinematic start, minimap, wrong way)
- Race-start cinematic: during the countdown the camera swings round the car from the front to the chase position (3 s), then the normal camera takes over.
- Minimap (right side under the camera buttons): road outline, start/finish dot, you (yellow) and the AI cars (red).
- WRONG WAY warning when you drive against the track direction.
- Still not done: bridges (the tracks are flat), motion blur (not available in the mobile renderer), LOD meshes, real GLB cars.

## Phase 12 adds (resolution scaling, desert dust)
- SETTINGS > RESOLUTION: AUTO (set by graphics quality) or 60 / 75 / 100% render scale. Lower it if the game stutters on your phone.
- Desert Run: cars kick up a sand-coloured dust trail above 45 km/h.

## Phase 13 adds (Time Trial ghost)
- TIME TRIAL now has a ghost: your fastest lap is recorded (20 samples a second, saved per track on the phone) and replayed as a transparent blue car on every later lap, so you can race your own best. The ghost appears after the first completed lap (or at once if a ghost was saved earlier). Ghosts are saved only for laps driven in Time Trial.
- Real car models: put a `.glb` named after the car id (for example `assets/cars/supercar.glb`) in `assets/cars/`; the game already loads it instead of the procedural body (see the "glb" fields in `scripts/car_data.gd`). No GLB files are included because they could not be downloaded here.

## Run
- PC: open folder in Godot 4.3, press F5. Keys: W/S gas/brake, A/D steer, Shift nitro, Space handbrake/drift, V change view, R reset, C next car.
- Android: push to GitHub, run the "Android Build" workflow (Actions tab), download the APK artifact.

## Using real 3D models (GLB)
Put the file in `assets/cars/` with the name used in `scripts/car_data.gd`
(e.g. `sports_coupe.glb`). It replaces the procedural body automatically.
Tweak `glb_scale`, `glb_rotation_y`, `glb_offset`; car must face -Z.
Free CC0 sources: kenney.nl (Car Kit), quaternius.com.

## Tuning (scripts/car_data.gd)
accel (m/s^2), max_speed (m/s), grip, brake. Suspension values are in `car_controller.gd`.
